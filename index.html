How It Works

Tag resources. Set a threshold.
Let IdleDown handle the rest.

No agents to install, no VPC peering, no complex IAM policies. Grant IdleDown read/start/stop permissions on your tagged resources and it does the rest.

Tag-based resource discovery

Tag your non-production resources with idledown:managed=true. IdleDown finds them automatically.

Idle detection

Monitors CPU, network I/O, and inbound connections. Hibernates automatically when below your threshold for N minutes.

Wake on demand

Slack command, web dashboard, or API. Resources back up in under 60 seconds. Your team barely notices.
